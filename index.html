Meet Nightily Furrying

Nightily Furrying is a mythical Beast that strongly resembles your typical Samoyed dog in shape and size,but with a few key differences.
You've probaly seen him before somewhere else with another mythical creature that is his companion,maybe during one of your C Programming contest.

What Does He Look Like?

Nightily has black fluffy fur and a long bushy tail with a patch of light blue fur on the tip that glows in the dark.A lot different from the usual white that normal Samoyed have.
He also can be seen always wearing a golden four-pointed star necklace around his neck,which is actually an old gift from his best friend.

What Does he Likes?

He likes a lot of things but you'll mainly see him do stuff like:

  • Programming (Hiscurrent passion)
  • Making music (Badly)
  • Drawing (Also Badly)
  • Gardening (Probaly badly too)

Some Quick Facts

Here are some interesting facts about Nightily:

  1. Can manipulate a lot of things (Doesn't know that yet).
  2. He tends to like flashy and complex stuff but has no skill to make them
  3. Get tired kinda easily.
  4. Had a lot of pets, they're no longer around.
